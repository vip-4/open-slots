Privacy & Fair Play

Data & privacy

Open Slots is a static browser demonstration. It has no user accounts, no login, no server-side processing, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party network requests. It does not set cookies and does not read or write local storage. Nothing you do on this page is transmitted anywhere.

All game assets — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG icons and the background image — are static files served by the hosting platform. Standard web-server access logs may be kept by the hosting provider under its own policy; the game itself collects nothing.

Fair play & play money

This is play money only. There is no real-money wagering, deposit, withdrawal, cash-out, prize, reward or conversion to anything of value. The on-screen jackpot counter is a decorative element inherited from the upstream demo and is not a balance.

The upstream project is a visual proof of concept. Symbols are selected uniformly at random and the game implements no paytable, no payline evaluation, no win calculation and no return-to-player (RTP) figure. It is not a gambling product and makes no promise of any outcome.

Audience

Intended for adults 18 and over. If you are under 18, please leave the site. If gambling is a concern for you or someone you know, please consult a local support service.
